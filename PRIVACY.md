# Breachcut Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 21, 2026_

Breachcut is a free Windows app that turns your Rainbow Six Siege clips into TikTok, YouTube Shorts and YouTube videos. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what we never touch.

## The short version

- **Your clips and videos stay on your PC.** Breachcut edits them on your computer. They are never uploaded to us.
- **When you sign in with Discord, we only get your Discord username, display name, avatar picture and a Discord user ID.** We don't get your password, messages, servers or friends list.
- **We don't sell your data, show ads, or share it with advertisers.**

## What we collect

When you sign in with Discord we store an account record with:

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Discord user ID, username, display name | To create your Breachcut account and show your name in the app |
| Discord avatar picture (link) | To show your picture in the app |
| Email address, if Discord shares it | Used only for account security and important notices. We never send marketing email without asking |
| App version and last time you opened Breachcut | To know which versions are still in use and plan updates |
| That you confirmed you are 13 or older | Required to use the service |

Breachcut also checks GitHub for updates when it opens. That request includes standard technical information (such as your IP address) that GitHub receives under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## What we never collect

- Your clips, videos, screen recordings or game footage
- Your Medal account, Ubisoft account or game data
- Your Discord password, messages, servers or friends
- Anything from inside Rainbow Six Siege. Breachcut does not read or change the game, and only works with video files already on your PC

## Where it's stored

Account data is stored with [Supabase](https://supabase.com), our database and sign-in provider, on servers in the United States. Sign-in is handled by Discord. Your sign-in session on your PC is encrypted with Windows' built-in data protection so only your Windows account can read it.

## Kids

Breachcut is not for children under 13. If you're under 13, please don't sign up. If we learn that a child under 13 has created an account, we'll delete it.

## Your choices

- **Sign out** any time from the profile menu in the app.
- **Delete your account:** contact us (below) and we'll delete your account record within 30 days. You can also remove Breachcut from your Discord account under *User Settings → Authorized Apps*.
- Uninstalling Breachcut removes the app's files from your PC. Your own clips and videos stay where they are.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and mention it in the app's update notes.

## Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at https://github.com/Fedr7/fed-clipper/issues

_Breachcut is not affiliated with Ubisoft, Medal or Discord._
